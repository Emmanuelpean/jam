The Code of Practice for the international recruitment of health and social care professionals in England ensures ethical international recruitment to the sector. Health and social care organisations in England do not actively recruit from those countries the World Health Organization (WHO) recognise as having the most pressing health and care workforce-related challenges unless there is government to government agreement to support managed recruitment activities. A list of these countries can be found in Annex A of the Code of Practice, red and amber list countries.

For additional support, the Department of Health and Social Care (DHSC) has published guidance for people from overseas who may want to work in health and social care in the UK.
